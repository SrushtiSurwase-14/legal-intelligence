Example & Partners LLP (fictional) - Client briefing: The Freedonia Shops Act explained, 10 June 2018

Under the Freedonia Shops and Establishments Act, 2010, no employee may work more than 9 hours in a day, and establishments employing 10 or more persons must register within 30 days.

Many employers also offer a 30 minute tea break during the working day. This is common practice, but the Act does not require a tea break.
