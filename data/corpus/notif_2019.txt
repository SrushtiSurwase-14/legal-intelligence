THE FREEDONIA SHOPS AND ESTABLISHMENTS (AMENDMENT) NOTIFICATION, 2019
Issued by the Ministry of Labour, Freedonia.

Section 1. Substitution of Section 4. For Section 4 of the principal Act, the following shall be substituted: No employee shall be required or allowed to work for more than 8 hours in a day.

Section 2. Commencement. This Notification comes into force on 1 April 2019.
