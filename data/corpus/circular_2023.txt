CIRCULAR NO. 3 OF 2023: CLARIFICATION ON OVERTIME COMPUTATION
Issued by the Office of the Labour Commissioner, Freedonia.

Section 1. Clarification. Overtime under Section 6 of the principal Act is payable for each day on which an employee works for more than 8 hours, and shall not be averaged over a week.

Section 2. Effect. This Circular does not change the rate of overtime wages.
