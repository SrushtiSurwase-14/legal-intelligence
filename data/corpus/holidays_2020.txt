THE FREEDONIA PUBLIC HOLIDAYS ORDER, 2020
Issued by the Ministry of Labour, Freedonia.

Section 1. Paid public holidays. Every employee in an establishment shall be entitled to 12 paid public holidays in a calendar year, as notified in the schedule.

Section 2. Repeal. The Freedonia Public Holidays Order, 2012 is repealed with effect from the commencement of this Order.
