THE NORTHERN PROVINCE SHOPS AND ESTABLISHMENTS RULES, 2021
Made by the Government of Northern Province, Freedonia.

Section 1. Short title. These Rules may be called the Northern Province Shops and Establishments Rules, 2021.

Section 2. Registration threshold. In Northern Province, every establishment employing 5 or more persons shall be registered with the Provincial Labour Officer within 30 days of commencement of its business.

Section 3. Display of notice. Every registered establishment shall display a notice of working hours at its entrance.
